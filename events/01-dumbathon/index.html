What it is

What is Dumbathon?

A hackathon open to every student, whatever their background.

about.exe

Some of the best ideas begin as ones that sound too silly to try.

The fear of looking foolish stops more people from creating than a lack of talent ever does. Dumbathon removes that fear.

  • 1
    Judgement-freeSilliness is a creative tool, so nobody fears looking foolish.

    Any backgroundYou don’t need to code. Every student can join a team.

    Pitch with confidenceSpot a problem, build fast and present it like you mean it.

One rule: be dumb.

Solve a real pain point on campus, in the funniest way you can.

> your mission

Imagine you are a Nottingham student who can start any startup or build anything for campus. Come up with the funniest solution and present it with full confidence.

Solve a pain point Obey the laws of physics AI tools allowed

Dumber add-onGilbert Premium: same buttons, but Gilbert ignores you faster.

How the event flows

From idea to pitch night.

Eight days, six steps, one very confident pitch.

  1. 1

    Welcome

    MON 30 NOV · 18:00

    You join on WhatsApp and get the assignment to brainstorm.

  2. 2

    Proposal report

    WED 2 DEC · 23:59

    A 2-page report: name, pain point, solution, customer.

  3. 3

    Shortlist

    THU 3 DEC · 18:00

    Shortlisted teams hear by email and start building.

  4. 4

    Build

    3 – 7 DEC

    Shortlisted teams make the prototype and slides online.

  5. 5

    Submit

    MON 7 DEC · 23:59

    Send your GitHub link, Vercel link and slides.

  6. 6

    Pitch night

    TUE 8 DEC · 17:00

    Pitch live at F1A15, get judged and win prizes.

Judging and prizes

How it’s judged.

Scored on the final pitch only. The top three teams share RM700.

  • 30%
    Creative AbsurdityHow original, funny and gloriously dumb the idea is.
  • 20%
    PracticalityDoes it solve the pain point, within the laws of physics?
  • 25%
    Product ExecutionDoes the prototype work, and are the links live?
  • 25%
    PresentationConfidence, delivery and quality of the slides.
