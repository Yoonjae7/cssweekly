WEEKLY #03 · Tuesday 13 October 2026

Induction committee briefing

Open deck →

Walked the committee through induction night: a 7–9 PM event for about 60 new students at six tables of ten, with three games, dinner, an internship panel and a group photo.

What we covered

  • Run of show: check-in, opening speech, committee and Junior Committee intros, games, then dinner, the internship panel and the group photo
  • Three games in one hour (7:30–8:30); scores add up and the top table wins the Gilbert figure and gets pizza first
  • Round 1 Caesar cipher (answer: DEBUG THE CODE) and Round 3 quiz show with Eashwar’s buzzer app
  • Roles: Selina as game master, Sakinah as emcee, one committee member per table
  • A 10-minute “Starting soon” countdown before Round 1
